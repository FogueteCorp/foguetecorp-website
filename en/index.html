About Foguete

I have practiced and taught capoeira for over a decade, trained across different groups and countries. Today I share this journey through classes, live shows, movement workshops, and a documentary project exploring combat cultures around the world.

01

Private Capoeira Classes

Learn capoeira adapted to your level, working on technique, body expression, and musicality in a personalized way.

Online Classes

Train from anywhere in the world with live follow-up and corrections.

In-Person & At-Home

Private classes at your space or outdoors, in San Sebastián de los Reyes and the surrounding Madrid area, for fast, safe progress focused on your goals.

02

Movement Workshops

Playful, dynamic sessions of capoeira, acrobatics, and balance for different age groups, plus one-off workshops for batizados and cord-changing ceremonies, festivals, and other capoeira events.

Kids

Children develop motor skills, rhythm, and musicality through play and capoeira.

Teens

Movement and discipline for teenagers, combining technique, acrobatics, martial art, and physical effort.

Adults / Beginners

A dedicated space for adults of all levels, adapted to their own pace and progression.

Competition Capoeira

Specific training focused on performance, game strategy, and preparation for competitive capoeira events.

Acrobatics & Balance

Classes focused on developing agility, body control, acrobatics, and improving dynamic balance.

Workshops & Events

Leading a workshop for your group, taking part in your roda or festival, or attending as a guest, I participate in capoeira events to fit whatever you need.

Live shows full of energy, live music, and high-impact acrobatics for festivals, celebrations, and private events.
